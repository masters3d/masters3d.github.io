## Brand Loyalty

In 1999 I fell in love with Sony. I had Sony headphones, Sony monitor, Sony sound system... Sony Sony Sony!! It was an obsession. In 2000 Sony came out with the color PDA, I was in a trance and I worked all summer to get it. It was 500 dollars, the Clié PEG-760. I remember I thought it was the greatest thing I had ever held in my hand. I would install about 50 games on it. In fact, I used it as a very expensive Game Boy. I was in love with Palm OS and I would check the website every day to see if a new version of their OS 6 would come out, but it never did. Then I bought the UX50, another 500 dollars. I was passionate about ClieSource.com and PalmInfoCenter.com. (I had the same experience when I bought my first Xbox and XboxScene.com). I would check these websites every 2 to 3 hours for news updates.

Then I moved on from Sony and Palm to the mobile revolution. I loved Google and I had T-Mobile so I got the G1. I liked it but it didn't live up to what I expected. About the same time my company gave me a BlackBerry and while it was a solid phone, it was not what I was looking for. So I switched to the iPhone and now I am happy.

## The Internet and Phones

I initially didn't understand why they made you get the internet with phones, but I actually like the fact that they do because the greatest thing about phones nowadays is the internet.

## My Favorite Applications

If you notice all of my favorite applications are online applications, so in reality I am not attached to my iPhone but my iPhone is just a gateway for those applications on the go.

## What I Am Looking For

The next evolution in mobile computing, whatever that may be.
