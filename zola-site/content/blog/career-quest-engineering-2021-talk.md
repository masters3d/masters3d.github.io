In November 2021 I gave a talk called "Career Quest Engineering," with a
companion deck I titled "Personal Journey." The slides are sparse, but the
speaker notes preserve the shape: video editor at a nonprofit, project manager
at a portable buildings company, software engineer at a tech company. A small
journey diagram I drew for it gives the same sequence in an even shorter form:
Media Producer, Video Editor, Project Manager, Software Engineer, then a future
retired self.

This is a Field Note because the artifact matters. The slides are not a polished
career theory. They are a 2021 snapshot of someone trying to explain why a path
that looked like several careers might still be one journey.

## The First Job Was Still About Making

The `Personal Journey` deck opens by asking, "What is your story?" The notes say
professional life is part of a life narrative, not the whole story, and that it
is up to you to control that narrative. Then the first career slide is Video
Editor at NonProfit.

The notes under that slide are concrete. The motivation was helping people in
need and getting other people to help. The ability was creating digital
artifacts that got more people involved. The learnings were how to work hard,
alignment to the mission, leadership, and confidence.

That matches the older blog post
[From Creative Tools to Software Engineering](/blog/from-creative-tools-to-software-engineering/),
where I wrote that I kept reaching for tools that let me make something that did
not exist before. Video editing was not a wrong turn away from software. It was
one form of the same making instinct. The medium was timelines, footage, and
digital artifacts instead of code.

The 2021 slides did not try to make that sound inevitable. They simply placed it
on the path. In the journey diagram, Video Editor is part of the career section,
not a preface to the real career. That distinction matters. It means the past
job was not erased once software engineering arrived.

## The Middle Step Changed the Question

The next slide is Project Manager at Portable Buildings Company. The notes are
more mixed. The motivation was that I was creating less and less digital content
and thought it might be better to switch to a career with better pay. The
ability was still work hard, leadership, and confidence, with support for sales
people selling more structures.

The learnings are the part that stayed with me: international travel is
overrated, a company should not be part of your identity, and money as a primary
motivation did not work for me.

Those are not universal laws. They are period details from that 2021 talk. They
record an earlier self testing a theory of work and discovering that the theory
was incomplete. Better pay and a different title did not answer the whole career
question. The talk was not saying money does not matter. It was saying that, for
this path, money alone was not enough to carry the story.

That middle step also explains why the word quest mattered. The
`Career Quest Engineering` deck defines quest through the root `quaere`, to ask
or inquire, and connects it to words like request, require, question, query, and
acquire. A career was not framed as a ladder in those slides. It was framed as a
search through time and place.

## Software Was Not the End of Angst

The final career slide is Software Engineer at Tech Company. The notes are short
and direct: motivation, I love to code. Ability, create clarity from ambiguity,
and all previous abilities. Learnings, more money does not remove career angst,
constant mastery is greater than arriving, and enjoy the journey is greater than
getting to a destination.

That is the line that makes the whole deck feel honest. Software engineering did
not fix the career question by becoming the final answer. It changed the shape
of the question. The work fit better because coding was something I loved, and
because the previous abilities came along: making artifacts, working hard,
leadership, confidence, support, and creating clarity from ambiguity. But even
there, the slide refuses the clean ending. More money did not remove career
angst. Mastery mattered more than arrival.

The `Career Quest Engineering` deck contains the early Quest Engine vocabulary:
career as movement, quest as inquiry, and engineering as operating the engine.
The wordplay is rough in places, but the attempt is clear: career is not only
the job title. It is the engineered motion of a professional life.

The talk also contains the beginnings of what later became more formal: Clear
Strategy, Objective Function, and Contextual Awareness. In 2021 the notes still
used motivation, ability, narrative, visibility, quality of life, and control.
The later framework would become more precise, but the concern was already
there. How do I make work enjoyable again? How do I know which part of the
engine needs a tune up when a project or career feels frustrating?

Returning to the source files now, I am struck by how much of the later blog was
already present in compressed form. The video editor did not disappear into the
software engineer. The project manager did not become a mistake to hide. Each
step contributed abilities and warnings. The 2021 talk was trying to preserve
that continuity while the path was still close enough to feel unsettled.

The deck also preserves the pandemic-era context without making the whole story
about the pandemic. The notes say I had started to feel disconnected with work
and worried about my career in those times. That matters because the talk was
not a victory lap after everything became clear. It was an attempt to regain a
sense of motion while the work felt disconnected. The framework grew from that
need for orientation, not from distance.

That is why the opening question still works: what is your story? In 2021, the
answer was not a perfect narrative. It was a sequence of roles, motivations,
abilities, and learnings, with software engineering as the current chapter
rather than the final proof. The useful record is that the journey did not
become meaningful because it arrived. It became meaningful because each step
clarified what kind of work was worth continuing to seek.
